Need help or found a bug? [Open an issue](https://github.com/jeremywho/stridering-site/issues) and describe what you saw, and on which device.

## Common questions

**The ring on my watch face is behind the app.**
watchOS limits how often a complication can refresh. StrideRing asks for an update about every 15 minutes, and opening the app updates it right away.

**My watch face shows the ring in a different color than I picked.**
Tinted watch faces and the iPhone Lock Screen draw every complication in the system's tint. Your chosen color appears in the app and on full-color faces.

**How do rest days work?**
After 6 goal days in a row you earn one rest day. If you then miss a day, the rest day is used automatically and your streak continues.

**Where do the steps come from?**
From Apple Health on your iPhone and Apple Watch. StrideRing shows the higher of Apple Health's total and your watch's live pedometer.
