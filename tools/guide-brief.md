# Tallyfoot guide brief

Every guide answers one question an Apple Watch or iPhone owner types into a search box, and answers it fully before it mentions Tallyfoot.

## Who reads it

Someone who wants to see or keep a daily step count, usually on Apple Watch. They found the page from a search, they have never heard of Tallyfoot, and they leave the moment the page reads like an ad.

## Rules

- Answer the question in the lede and the first section. A reader who stops there must have their answer.
- Write only what you can back up. Every claim about watchOS, iOS, Apple Health, or another app needs a page you fetched in this run, listed in `sources`. Drop any claim you could not confirm.
- Claims about Tallyfoot come only from the App Store listing included below. Never invent a feature, number, or setting.
- Mention Tallyfoot at most twice in the body, where it is an honest answer to the question. Link it as `[Tallyfoot](STORE_URL)`; the page adds the App Store button itself.
- Name other apps fairly, with their real pricing as fetched. Never call one bad.
- Plain sentences. No hype words, invented figures, testimonials, emoji, or exclamation marks.
- Link other tallyfoot.com pages as `[text]({{ "/path" | relative_url }})`, never a bare `/path`, so the GitHub Pages mirror works too.
- Use `##` section headings, short paragraphs, and numbered lists for steps. No `#` heading: the page prints the title.
- 600 to 1,200 words of body.

## Topic backlog

Pick topics only the developer of a step-goal app can answer well, and skip any that an existing guide already covers.

- Why Apple Watch and iPhone show different step counts, and which one Apple Health keeps
- How to see your step count on the iPhone Lock Screen
- How to keep a step streak through a sick day or a rest day
- How to set a daily step goal you will actually keep
- Why a step complication shows a different number than the Activity app
- How to track steps on iPhone without an Apple Watch
- How to move your step history to a new iPhone or Apple Watch
