---
title: Tallyfoot Privacy Policy
---

*Effective November 3, 2026*

Tallyfoot doesn't collect any data about you. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and the app makes no network connections to the developer or anyone else.

## Step data

- **What's read:** with your permission, Tallyfoot reads your step count from Apple Health on your iPhone and Apple Watch, and from your devices' motion sensors. It only reads. It never writes to Apple Health.
- **Where it stays:** step counts, your daily totals, your starting streak and your streak history stay on your own devices. They're shared only between your own Apple Watch and iPhone, using Apple's built-in WatchConnectivity link. Nothing is sent to the developer or to any third party.
- **Backups:** Tallyfoot marks its step data to be excluded from device backups.
- **Settings:** your daily goal and ring color are stored as app settings on your devices and are included in your normal device backups.

## Notifications

Tallyfoot can show one local notification when you reach your daily goal. It's generated on your watch, and nothing about it leaves your devices.

## Deleting your data

Deleting Tallyfoot from your iPhone and Apple Watch removes everything it stored. Your step data in Apple Health isn't affected.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
